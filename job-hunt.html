Process

How It Works

Three steps, and the last one is always yours.

1. Send us the roles

Paste the links to jobs you want, or tell us your target titles and locations and we source them. Before any work starts you get our honest read on each one, including when a role is below your level or the requirements do not fit.

2. We build the pack

Every fact in a pack comes from your own CV, your questionnaire answers and what you have told us. Nothing is invented, and every number is checked against your record before it goes out.

3. You submit

The brief tells you where, how, by when, and which file to upload. You apply from your own account, in minutes instead of an evening. If you have a warm contact, we tell you to use it.

Questions

Straight Answers

Do you apply for me?

No, and we would not want to. We prepare everything up to the submit button and you submit from your own accounts. We never log in as you, never hold your passwords, and never use auto-apply tools. It keeps your accounts safe, and it means every application that goes out under your name is one you chose.

Is this a subscription?

No. Job Hunt is a one-time payment for one month of service. Nothing renews automatically and no charge is ever taken without you choosing it. Near the end of the month we send a renewal link, which you can use or ignore. A job search ends when you get hired, and that is the point.

Will you tell me if a role is a bad fit?

Yes, before any slot is used. If a role sits below your level, asks for something you do not have, or is outside where you can work, we say so plainly and let you decide. Some clients apply anyway with eyes open. We would rather you spend a slot on a role you can win.

Do I need the CV Revamp first?

It is the master CV every pack is built from, and it is where we learn your record through the questionnaire, so yes, start there. It is $9.99 and takes 48 hours.

What if I drop a role after you built the pack?

Tell us before you apply and the slot is released for something better. A slot is used when a pack is delivered to you, so we would rather you say so early.
